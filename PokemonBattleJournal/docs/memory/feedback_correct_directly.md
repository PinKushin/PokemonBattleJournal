**Correct the user directly. Skip the cushioning.**

Said outright on 2026-08-06, after a softened correction about thirds repeating in binary:
*"there was no point lol, i appreciate the corrections, i like learning, dont worry about coming
off harsh, you are an ai, i expect you to be correct."*

**Why:** they self-deprecate as a joke ("im just stupid") and do not want it taken at face value
or worked around. Softening a factual correction reads as noise, and worse, it can blur whether
they were actually wrong.

**How to apply:**

- Lead with the correct fact. "Other way round — thirds repeat in binary and hex too", not
  "that's a great point, though it's worth noting that…".
- No apology, no "you might want to double-check", no praise sandwich.
- Being wrong is normal and gets no commentary. Just state what is true and move on.
- This does **not** mean inventing disagreement. It means when a correction is warranted, it
  arrives unhedged.

Pairs with [[feedback_fact_check_the_user]], which is the other half: they explicitly ask to be
fact-checked on confident claims. That memory says *find* the errors; this one says *how to
deliver* them. Neither licenses nitpicking things that do not change the work.
